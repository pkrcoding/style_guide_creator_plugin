# Logo

Our logo is the fastest way city teams, utilities, investors and partners recognise Northwind Labs. Using it the same way every time builds trust, and that matters when people rely on our data for public decisions.

## Our logo

The Northwind Labs logo is a **combination mark**: a symbol and a wordmark that always appear in a fixed relationship.

- **The symbol** is a Fjord Teal circle holding an open Daybreak Amber peak, an upward-pointing chevron with its base cut away.
- **The wordmark** is "Northwind" in a heavy, geometric lowercase sans-serif in Polar Night, followed by "LABS" in small, widely spaced capitals in Fjord Teal.

The form sets the tone for the whole identity. Sharp apexes inside a soft circle become our mix of precise and approachable. The heavy wordmark carries our confidence. The upward peak points forward, which matches our optimism. The cool teal and navy are lifted by one warm accent. Our layout, icon, chart and motion rules all come from these cues.

## Anatomy

| Part | What it is | Can it be used alone? |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol | Teal circle with the amber open peak | Yes, as an avatar, favicon or app icon, or where space is square |
| Wordmark "Northwind" | Heavy lowercase name in Polar Night | No. Never separate it from the symbol |
| Descriptor "LABS" | Spaced capitals in Fjord Teal, on the wordmark's baseline | No. Never remove, move or resize it |

The logo is wide, so it needs the symbol for square spaces. There is no tagline lock-up. If one is needed later, ask the Marketing team for designed artwork; do not set a tagline next to the logo yourself.

## Versions and when to use each

Use full colour wherever it passes. Use a single-colour version only when the background or the production method needs it.

| Version | Use it for | File |
|---|---|---|
| Primary, full colour | Default on white and light neutral backgrounds: websites, documents, slides, reports | `primary-full-colour` |
| Symbol, full colour | Social avatars, favicons, app icons, small square spaces | `symbol-full-colour` |
| Single colour, dark (knockout) | Light backgrounds where colour is not possible, and Daybreak Amber backgrounds; one-colour print, engraving, fax and stamps | `primary-mono-dark-knockout` |
| Single colour, light (knockout) | Fjord Teal, Polar Night and dark backgrounds, and calm, dark areas of photographs | `primary-mono-light-knockout` |

Our single-colour versions use the **knockout** style: the circle is solid and the peak is cut out, so the background shows through. We tested a plain single-colour fill, and the peak merged into a solid disc and the mark was lost. Always use the knockout files for single-colour work.

## Placement

- **Web and apps:** top left in the header, aligned to the left margin of the grid in chapter 6, and linked to the home page.
- **Documents, reports and letterhead:** top left on the cover or first page. Repeat it only in the footer of later pages, at a smaller size.
- **Slides:** the full logo on title and end slides; the symbol, or nothing, on content slides.
- **Social and video:** the symbol as the avatar; the full logo on end cards, placed bottom right or centred.
- **Campaigns:** choose one corner per campaign and keep it the same on every asset.

Align the logo's left edge with the edge of the circle, not the clear-space box, so it lines up optically with the text below it.

- **White and light neutral:** full colour.
- **Fjord Teal:** light single-colour version. Full colour makes the circle disappear into the background.
- **Polar Night and dark neutral:** light single-colour version. Full colour loses the wordmark.
- **Daybreak Amber:** dark single-colour version. Full colour reaches only 2.9:1 here, just below 3:1, so we do not allow it.
- **Photographs:** place the logo only on a calm, even area, such as sky, water or a plain wall. Use the light version on dark areas and the dark version on light areas. If no area is calm enough, put the logo on a solid panel in a brand colour, with full clear space inside the panel.
- **Patterns and charts:** never place the logo over patterns, maps, charts or texture.

> **Do** use the full-colour logo on white and light neutral backgrounds.

> **Don't** use the full-colour logo on Daybreak Amber, Fjord Teal or Polar Night. Use the single-colour knockout version shown in the table.

## Misuse

Never change the logo artwork. These rules protect it:

1. Don't stretch, squash or change its proportions.
2. Don't rotate or tilt the logo or the peak.
3. Don't recolour the logo, or any part of it, outside the approved versions.
4. Don't add effects such as shadows, glows, bevels, gradients or outlines.
5. Don't rearrange, resize or space out the symbol, "Northwind" or "LABS".
6. Don't place the logo on busy photos, patterns or low-contrast backgrounds.
7. Don't put the logo inside shapes, badges or frames that are not in this guide.
8. Don't use old, redrawn or traced versions. Download the current files.
9. Don't type "Northwind Labs" in another font next to the symbol in place of the wordmark.
10. Don't crop the logo or let it bleed off the edge of a page or screen.
11. Don't use the plain single-colour fill that hides the peak. Use the knockout version.
12. Don't turn the peak into an arrow, bullet or decorative pattern.

> **Warning** Anything not covered here needs approval from the Marketing team before production.

## Files and naming

| Format | Use it for |
|---|---|
| SVG | Websites, apps, digital design tools |
| PDF or EPS (vector) | Print, signage, merchandise, embroidery briefs |
| PNG (transparent) | Office tools such as Word, PowerPoint and Google Slides, and email |

Name exported files `northwind-labs-logo-{version}-{colour}.{ext}`, for example `northwind-labs-logo-primary-fullcolour.svg` or `northwind-labs-logo-symbol-light.png`. Never rename files with dates or "final". Use the version number in the download pack instead. All files are in the downloads section of chapter 20.

> **Note** The current single-colour files are made automatically from the master artwork. For print, embroidery and signage, ask the Marketing team for the designer-drawn single-colour master.

## Logo accessibility
- **Alt text:** use the alt text in the specification table. When the logo links to our home page, use "Northwind Labs home".
- **Never alone:** always state the organisation's name in page titles, document properties and running text. Never use an image of the logo in place of the name in a sentence.
- **Contrast:** the logo keeps at least 3:1 against its background on every use. This is our own rule, even though WCAG exempts logos.
- **Decorative repeats:** when the logo appears more than once on a page, for example in a footer, give the repeat empty alt text so screen readers do not announce it twice.
