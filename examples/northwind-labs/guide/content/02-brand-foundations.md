# Brand foundations

Everything else in this guide, from the colour of a chart to the wording of an error message, should trace back to this chapter. Northwind Labs works in climate data analytics for city governments and utilities, where people make long-lived, public, expensive decisions. Our foundations exist so that every audience, from a utility engineer checking a load profile to an investor reading a board pack, meets the same organisation: confident, precise and optimistic.

> **Note** Northwind Labs did not supply a mission, vision, values, positioning or tagline. Every statement marked *Proposed — confirm with leadership:* is a draft written by the brand team from the inputs we received: the industry, the audiences, the personality ("confident, precise, optimistic") and the logo. Leadership should edit and approve these drafts before the guide is published. Do not quote them externally until they are approved.

## Mission

The mission says what we do, for whom and why, in one sentence.

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* **We turn climate data into clear, trustworthy evidence, so city governments and utilities can make better decisions for the places and people they serve.**

## Vision

The vision is the future we are working towards. It should stay true for years, even as our products change.

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* **A future where every city and utility can plan, invest and act on climate with confidence, because the evidence is clear to everyone who relies on it.**

## Positioning

Positioning tells the team where we fit in the market. It is an internal tool, not a line to publish.

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* For city sustainability officers and utility engineers who must make high-stakes climate decisions with complex and incomplete data, Northwind Labs is the climate data analytics partner that turns that data into clear, decision-ready evidence, because we show our methods, our assumptions and our uncertainty, not just our answers.

> **Warning** The "because" in a positioning statement must be provably true. Before using this proof point in sales or investor material, confirm that our products and reports really do publish methods, assumptions and uncertainty ranges. If they do not yet, change the proof point rather than the product description.

## Values

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* Four values, each with the behaviour that shows it. A value without a behaviour is a slogan, so keep the behaviour line when you quote a value.

| Value | What it means | How it shows up in our work |
|---|---|---|
| Show our working | Trust in climate data is earned through transparency. | We publish our methods, data sources, assumptions and uncertainty alongside every result. |
| Evidence before claims | We never claim more impact than the data supports. | We say "estimated", give ranges, and cite the source for every number we publish. |
| Clear for everyone | A result is only useful if the people who act on it can understand it. | We write a plain-language summary for every technical output, and we design to WCAG 2.2 AA. |
| Progress together | Climate action is shared work between cities, utilities, partners and residents. | We credit partners and customers accurately, and we share what we learn, including what did not work. |

## Audiences

Our four audiences have different expertise, but they share one need: evidence they can trust and act on. Write for the least specialist reader who will see the content, and offer more depth for those who want it.

| Audience | Who they are | What they need from us | Main channels | Accessibility and inclusion needs to plan for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| City sustainability officers | Public-sector staff who set and report on climate targets, often explaining results to elected members and residents | Clear findings they can reuse in council papers, honest uncertainty, and progress they can report | Website, LinkedIn, email, webinars, reports | Public bodies must meet accessibility regulations, so anything they republish must be accessible (tagged PDFs, alt text, plain-language summaries). Their own readers include residents with a wide range of reading levels and first languages. |
| Utility engineers | Technical staff who plan, operate and maintain networks and assets | Correct units, explicit methods, data definitions, and outputs that fit their tools | Product, documentation, technical notes, YouTube walkthroughs | Precise, scannable content. Engineers often read on site or on mobile, so tables must reflow and charts must not rely on colour alone. |
| Investors | Current and prospective funders assessing our business and its impact | Verifiable metrics, clear risks, and no overstated climate impact | Investor updates, website, LinkedIn, board packs | Accessible PDFs and slide decks, consistent figures across documents, and plain summaries of technical points. |
| Climate-tech partners | Organisations that integrate with, resell or co-deliver with us | Clear roles, accurate joint claims, integration detail and brand rules for co-branding | Partner pages, LinkedIn, X, events, documentation | Many partners read English as an additional language, so avoid idioms, sports metaphors and culturally specific references. |

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* We also treat **residents and elected members** as a secondary audience. They do not buy from us, but they read the reports our customers publish, so our charts and summaries must make sense to them too.

## Personality

The organisation chose three personality traits. Each one drives a voice attribute in chapter 11.

| Trait | What it looks like | Linked voice attribute |
|---|---|---|
| Confident | We state findings directly and stand behind our methods. The heavy wordmark and upward peak in our logo carry this. | Confident, never arrogant |
| Precise | We use the right number, the right unit and the right level of certainty. The logo's sharp geometric apexes carry this. | Precise, never pedantic |
| Optimistic | We focus on what can be done and the progress that is possible, without hiding risk. The warm Daybreak Amber peak carries this. | Optimistic, never hyped |

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* A fourth voice attribute, **Clear**, supports all three traits and our accessibility commitment. It is listed in chapter 11 as a voice attribute rather than a personality trait.

## Tagline

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* **Clear data for climate decisions.**

Alternatives for leadership to consider: "Climate data, made clear." and "See the evidence. Make the call."

> **Warning** Run a trademark and domain search before adopting any tagline. Do not use a tagline that promises an outcome we cannot prove, such as "Cutting city emissions".

## Boilerplate

Use the boilerplate at the end of press releases, partner announcements, event listings and profiles. Copy it exactly, and choose the length that fits the space.

> **Note** These drafts contain no facts about our history, customers or results because none were supplied. Add verified facts (for example, the year we were founded or where we are based) only after leadership confirms them.

### Short (about 25 words)

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* Northwind Labs turns climate data into clear, decision-ready evidence for city governments and utilities, so they can plan, invest and act with confidence.

### Medium (about 50 words)

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* Northwind Labs is a climate data analytics company working with city governments and utilities. We turn complex climate and energy data into clear evidence that sustainability officers, engineers and investors can act on. We show our methods and our uncertainty, so every decision rests on evidence people can check.

### Long (about 100 words)

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* Northwind Labs is a climate data analytics company working with city governments and utilities. Cities and utilities face long-term, high-stakes decisions about energy, infrastructure and emissions, often with complex and incomplete data. We turn that data into clear, decision-ready evidence that sustainability officers, utility engineers, investors and partners can understand and act on. We show our methods, our assumptions and our uncertainty, so every result can be checked and explained to the people it affects. We believe better evidence leads to better climate decisions. To find out more, email hello@northwindlabs.example.

> **Do** check that the data domains in the boilerplate (climate, energy, emissions, infrastructure) match what our products actually cover before publishing.

> **Don't** add customer names, savings figures, emissions reductions or awards to the boilerplate unless they are verified, current and approved for public use.

> **Accessibility** When the boilerplate appears in a PDF or slide, keep it as live text, not an image, so screen readers and translation tools can read it.
