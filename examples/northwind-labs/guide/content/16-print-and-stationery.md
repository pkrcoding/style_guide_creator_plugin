# Print and stationery

Printed pieces travel into council chambers, utility control rooms and investor meetings, often without us there to explain them. They must look as precise as our data and be readable by everyone, including people with low vision reading a tender pack under office lighting.

Our default market is the UK, so all specifications use A-series paper and UK sizes. Order print through the Marketing team (hello@northwindlabs.example), who hold the master artwork and approve proofs.

## Business cards

**Size:** 85 × 55 mm (UK standard), landscape, with 3 mm bleed and a 4 mm safe margin inside the trim.

| Side | Content |
|---|---|
| Front | Primary full-colour logo on white, top left, about 40 mm wide, with full clear space (chapter 3) |
| Front | Name in Manrope bold, job title in Inter regular, then direct phone, email and [Website], left-aligned to the logo's left edge |
| Back | Solid Fjord Teal or Polar Night with the single-colour light logo or the symbol, centred |

- Information hierarchy: name, job title, email, phone, web address. Leave out anything a person will not use.
- Minimum text size is 8pt, including contact details. Reversed (light on dark) text is at least 8pt and never in a light weight.
- Use a matt or uncoated stock of 400 gsm or heavier. Matt stock reduces glare and takes handwriting.
- A QR code is optional. If you use one, print the short web address next to it.

> **Do** keep the front white with the full-colour logo; it is the most recognisable version.

> **Don't** print the full-colour logo on the Fjord Teal or Polar Night side: the teal circle disappears. Use the single-colour light logo.

## Letterhead

**Size:** A4 (210 × 297 mm). Margins of at least 20 mm on all sides.

- Logo: primary full-colour, top left, about 50 mm wide, aligned to the left text margin.
- Footer: a single line or two-column block in Inter at 8pt, in Polar Night: `Northwind Labs | [Address line] | [Town] [Postcode] | hello@northwindlabs.example | [Website]`.
- If Northwind Labs is a UK registered company, the registered name, company number, place of registration and registered office address must appear on business letters. *Proposed — confirm with leadership:* add `[Registered company name], registered in [England and Wales], company number [number], registered office [address]` in the footer at 8pt minimum.
- Body copy: Inter (or the office substitute in chapter 17) at 11pt, left-aligned, ragged right, never justified.
- Keep a **digital letterhead** as a Word template (`.dotx`) built with real styles (chapter 17). Most letters are now sent as PDF; export them as tagged PDFs.
- Print the pre-printed version on 100–120 gsm uncoated paper that works with office laser printers.

## Envelopes and compliment slips

| Item | Size | Logo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| DL envelope | 110 × 220 mm | Symbol or primary logo, top left of the flap side or front, at least 30 mm from the address area | Fits A4 folded in three; standard UK business envelope |
| C5 envelope | 162 × 229 mm | Primary logo top left | A4 folded once |
| C4 envelope | 229 × 324 mm | Primary logo top left | A4 flat, for tender packs and reports |
| Compliment slip | 99 × 210 mm (⅓ A4) | Primary logo top left | Contact line in the footer as on the letterhead |

- Keep the address window, postage area and the bottom 15 mm clear. Royal Mail sorting machines read these areas; check the current Royal Mail guidance with your printer.
- Return address on the back flap: `Northwind Labs, [Address line], [Town] [Postcode]`.

## Brochures, flyers and reports

- Build A4 pages on a 12-column grid with 15–20 mm outer margins and a 5 mm gutter, following the composition principles in chapter 6.
- Bleed is 3 mm on every edge. Keep text and logos at least 5 mm inside the trim.
- Place the logo on the front cover bottom left or top left, and on the back cover with the contact details. Use one placement per document series and keep it consistent across a campaign.
- Covers can use a large Fjord Teal or Polar Night panel. Keep to the colour proportions in chapter 4: mostly white and neutral, with Daybreak Amber used for small highlights only.
- Headlines in Manrope, body in Inter at 10–12pt, charts following chapter 9.
- Images are 300 ppi at final size. Logos and type are always vector (PDF or EPS), never pasted screenshots.

## Posters and large format

- Common sizes: A3, A2, A1, A0. Scale the logo to roughly one-sixth to one-quarter of the poster width.
- One message, one headline. The headline should be readable from 3 m on an A2 poster: at least 72pt.
- Keep the bottom 10% clear for the logo, the call to action and the short web address.

## Print colour and paper

- Supply artwork in CMYK for process print. The CMYK values in chapter 4 are a starting conversion: ask the printer for a hard proof on the actual stock before every first run.
- Pantone (spot) values must be matched from a physical Pantone swatch book under daylight conditions. Coated (C) and uncoated (U) swatches differ visibly, so specify which one the job uses.
- Fjord Teal and Polar Night lose saturation on uncoated stock. Check the proof against the previous approved print, not against a screen.
- Large Polar Night areas: ask the printer for a rich black-style build based on Polar Night so it does not look grey. Small text stays in a single solid colour to avoid misregistration.
- Paper: matt, silk or uncoated finishes. Avoid high gloss on anything people read, because it reflects light. Choose FSC-certified or recycled stock; it suits a climate business.

## Accessible print

- Body text at least 11pt, ideally 12pt, for reports, letters and tender documents.
- Use the approved text pairings in chapter 4 only. Daybreak Amber is never used for text on white or light paper; use `accent-600` if you need amber text.
- Never place text over a busy photograph. Use a solid panel behind it.
- Left-aligned text, no justified paragraphs, no long passages in capitals or italics.
- Every QR code has a printed short web address next to it, so people without a smartphone can still follow it.
- Offer a **large-print version** (at least 16pt body, same content, simplified layout) and an accessible digital version on request. State this on the back cover: "This document is available in large print and accessible formats. Email hello@northwindlabs.example."

> **Do** order a hard proof on the final paper before any run over 250 copies.

> **Don't** approve colour from a screen or a PDF alone; screens are not calibrated to print.

## Files and handover to printers

- Send print-ready PDF (PDF/X-4 is a good default) with crop marks, 3 mm bleed and fonts embedded.
- Name files `northwind-labs-[item]-[size]-[version].pdf`, for example `northwind-labs-business-card-85x55-v2.pdf`.
- Download master logo files from the asset library listed in chapter 20.
