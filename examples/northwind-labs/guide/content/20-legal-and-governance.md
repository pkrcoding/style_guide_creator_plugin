# Legal, governance and assets

A brand only stays consistent if someone owns it, the right files are easy to find, and legal lines are used correctly. This chapter covers trademarks, copyright, licences, approvals, the asset library and how the guide is kept up to date.

## Trademarks

*Proposed — confirm with legal counsel:* we have no record of registered trademarks for the Northwind Labs name or logo. Until legal counsel confirms registration status in each market:

- Do not use the ® symbol. It may only be used for marks registered in the relevant jurisdiction, and misuse can be an offence in the UK.
- Use ™ only if counsel advises it, and then only on the first or most prominent use of the logo or name in a piece, not on every instance.
- Never add a symbol inside the logo artwork. Place any mark outside the clear space, set in the body typeface at a small size.

When writing about the company, always use the full name "Northwind Labs" on first mention, with a capital N and L. Never abbreviate it to "NWL", hyphenate it or write it as one word.

> **Do** write "Northwind Labs" in plain text in body copy, so screen readers and search engines read it correctly.

> **Don't** use the logo in place of the name in a sentence.

## Copyright lines

Use this line on reports, websites, presentations and templates: "© [Year] Northwind Labs. All rights reserved." *Proposed — confirm the registered legal entity name with legal counsel and replace "Northwind Labs" with it if it differs.* For open data or reports we want shared widely, legal counsel may approve a Creative Commons licence instead. Name the licence and link to it in full.

## Font, image and data licences

| Asset | Source | Licence | What it allows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manrope (display) | Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Free use in print, web, apps, video and social; may be embedded |
| Inter (body) | Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | As above |
| JetBrains Mono (data and code) | Google Fonts | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | As above |
| Stock photography and illustration | Per supplier | Per image | Check each licence covers the channel, territory and duration |
| Third-party data and maps | Per provider | Per dataset | Credit the source as the provider requires, on the chart or map itself |

Keep licence records for every stock image, music track and dataset in the asset library alongside the file. Never use an image found on a search engine or social media without written permission.

## Approvals

| What you are making | Who approves | Typical turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Social posts using templates | Social lead in the Marketing team | 1 working day |
| New campaign, website page or report design | Marketing team brand owner | 3 working days |
| Co-branded material with a council, utility or partner | Marketing team brand owner, plus the partner's own brand team | 5 working days |
| Public environmental or performance claims | Marketing team brand owner, plus legal review | 5 working days |
| Any change to the logo, colours or typefaces | Leadership sign-off via the Marketing team | Not routine |

*Proposed — confirm with leadership:* turnaround times above are a starting point.

> **Do** submit work with its alt text, captions and contrast checks, so the review can cover accessibility too.

> **Don't** publish data-led claims about emissions or savings without a named, checkable source.
