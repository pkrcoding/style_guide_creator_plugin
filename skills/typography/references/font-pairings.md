# Font pairings (free, SIL Open Font License unless noted)

All of these fonts are on Google Fonts and are free for commercial use, including web, print, apps, social and video, under the SIL OFL 1.1. Verify the licence file shipped with the download. Pick by matching the logo's personality cues.

| Logo personality | Display | Body | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geometric, modern, tech | Plus Jakarta Sans | Inter | Geometric display; Inter is highly legible with a large x-height and tabular figures |
| Geometric, friendly, rounded | Nunito | Nunito Sans | Rounded terminals echo soft logos; the sans body stays crisp |
| Neutral, corporate, trustworthy | IBM Plex Sans | IBM Plex Sans | One family, wide language support, clear I/l/1 |
| Humanist, warm, public sector or charity | Source Sans 3 | Source Sans 3 | Open apertures, excellent at small sizes, broad language coverage |
| Accessibility-first | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next | Designed for low-vision readers with highly distinct letterforms |
| Editorial, heritage, premium | Fraunces | Source Serif 4 or Inter | Characterful serif display; readable body |
| Classic, institutional (education, law, finance) | Libre Baskerville or Crimson Pro | Source Sans 3 | Traditional serif headlines, sans body for screens |
| Bold, energetic, sport, youth | Archivo (Expanded or Black) | Archivo | Strong widths for impact; same family keeps harmony |
| Playful, creative, consumer | Fredoka or Baloo 2 | Nunito Sans | Expressive display; restrained body |
| Technical, data-heavy | Space Grotesk | IBM Plex Sans; IBM Plex Mono for data | Distinctive but functional |
| Multilingual (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Devanagari, CJK) | Noto Sans (+ script-specific Noto) | Noto Sans | Designed for very broad script coverage with consistent metrics |

Monospace: JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono or Source Code Pro.

## System fallback stacks

- Sans: `system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`
- Serif: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`
- Mono: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`

## Office and email substitutes

| Brand style | Office substitute | Email-safe stack |
|---|---|---|
| Geometric or neutral sans | Arial, Calibri or Aptos | Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif |
| Humanist sans | Segoe UI or Calibri | 'Segoe UI', Arial, sans-serif |
| Serif | Georgia or Cambria | Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif |

## Commercial fonts

If the organisation uses commercial fonts (e.g. from Monotype, Adobe Fonts or independent foundries), the licence usually separates desktop, web (by page views), app embedding, ePub and broadcast. The guide must say which licences the organisation holds and who to contact before using the font in a new medium. Do not assume Adobe Fonts covers logo creation or app embedding.
