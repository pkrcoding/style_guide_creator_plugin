# Voice, tone and writing style

Load **brand-standards** first. Read `references/writing-style.md` for the default editorial rules.

## Sources, in priority order

1. What the user told you (quote it faithfully).
2. The organisation's website or materials (facts stated there).
3. The logo's personality cues and the industry. Use these only for **proposals**, which are clearly marked.

Never invent facts, figures, history, awards or claims. Proposed mission, values or taglines are labelled `*Proposed — confirm with leadership:*` and listed as assumptions in your handoff.

## 02 Brand foundations

- **Mission** (what we do, for whom, why, in one sentence), **vision** (the future we work toward), **positioning** ("For [audience] who [need], [org] is the [category] that [benefit], because [proof]").
- **Values:** three to five, each with a one-line behaviour ("We say what we mean: plain words, no jargon").
- **Audiences:** for each, who they are, what they need from us, the channels they use, and any accessibility needs to plan for (e.g. older audiences, low digital literacy, multilingual communities).
- **Personality:** three to five traits that link to the voice attributes in chapter 11.
- **Tagline** (if any) and **boilerplate** at about 25, 50 and 100 words.

Propose `foundations.*` values in your handoff.

## 11 Voice and tone

- **Voice attributes:** three or four. For each, give a "we are / we are not" pair and an example sentence. E.g. Confident (we are: clear and direct; we are not: arrogant).
- **Tone by context:** a table with the situation, the tone dial (more formal ↔ more casual, more serious ↔ lighter), an example, and words to avoid. Cover marketing, website, product/UI microcopy, customer support, error messages, bad news and crisis, social posts, social replies and complaints, recruitment, investor/legal, and internal communications.
- **Before / after rewrites:** at least four short ones, in the organisation's own domain.
- **Words we use and avoid:** a short glossary with preferred terms.

## 12 Writing style

Adapt `references/writing-style.md` to `meta.locale`. Cover the brand name (capitalisation, possessive, never abbreviate unless defined), spelling, grammar and punctuation choices (serial comma, quotation marks, dashes), capitalisation (sentence case for headings and buttons), numbers, dates, times, currency, phone numbers and addresses, abbreviations and acronyms, links and calls to action, lists, **plain language** (target reading age, sentence length, active voice), **inclusive language** (people-first or identity-first as the community prefers, gender-neutral terms, avoiding ableist metaphors and idioms such as "blind spot" or "falling on deaf ears", age, race and culture), and **accessible content** (descriptive links, meaningful headings, emoji and hashtag rules, no directional-only instructions like "click the green button on the right").
