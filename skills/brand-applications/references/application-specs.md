## Print production

- Bleed: 3 mm (0.125 in). Safe zone: 3–5 mm inside the trim.
- Resolution: 300 ppi at final size for images; vector for logos and type.
- Colour: CMYK for process print; Pantone for spot colours. Proof on the actual stock. Coated (C) and uncoated (U) swatches differ visibly.
- Minimum type: 7pt for legal text and business cards; 9–12pt body; 16pt or more for large-print editions.
- Reversed (light on dark) type: at least 8pt, and avoid thin weights and serifs that fill in on uncoated stock.
- Rich black for large areas (e.g. C60 M40 Y40 K100, printer-dependent). 100% K for small text.

## Signage (check local regulations)

- Letter height for reading distance: about 25 mm of cap height per 10 m of viewing distance (rule of thumb). Directional signs at least 15 mm cap height at close range.
- Contrast: aim for an LRV difference of at least 70 points between text and sign background, and sign and wall where required (e.g. UK BS 8300 guidance).
- Tactile and braille where regulations require them (e.g. US ADA Standards §703 for permanent room identification).
- Mounting heights: follow local codes (e.g. ADA: tactile characters 48–60 in above the floor to the baseline).
- Use sentence case, sans-serif, and a non-reflective (matt) finish.
