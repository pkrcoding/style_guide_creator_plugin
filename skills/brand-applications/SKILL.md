# Brand applications

Load **brand-standards** first. Standard sizes and specs are in `references/application-specs.md`. Every application must use the approved logo versions, colour pairings, type tokens and accessibility rules. Reference them by name rather than restating values.

## 14 Digital and web

Keep `{{favicons}}`. Cover: the website header and footer (logo placement and size, linking the logo home with alt "[Org] home"), buttons (primary = `surface-brand` + `text-on-brand`; secondary; text links underlined), form fields (visible labels, 3:1 borders, error text with icon), states (hover, focus, active, disabled; disabled is not the only signal), the focus style (cross-reference chapter 13), favicons and app icons (sizes, maskable safe zone, `<head>` snippet), Open Graph images, **email templates** (600px width, live text not images, alt text, at least 14px text, 44px tap targets, dark-mode safe logos with a transparent PNG and padding), **email signatures** (name, role, pronouns optional, phone, a small logo image with alt text or text-only, no image-only signatures, no quotes or banners unless approved), and video (title cards, lower thirds, captions style, end cards, thumbnail rules).

## 16 Print and stationery

Cover: business cards (sizes per market, the information hierarchy, minimum 7pt text, matt stock for readability), letterhead (A4 or US Letter, margins, logo position, digital template version), envelopes (DL, C4, C5, #10), compliment slips, brochures, flyers and posters (grid, bleed 3mm or 0.125in, safe margin), print colour (CMYK conversions to verify, Pantone matching, coated vs uncoated, proofing), paper (matt or silk to reduce glare), large-print versions on request (at least 16pt), and accessible print (at least 11–12pt body, contrast, no text over busy images, a QR code with a written short URL alongside).

## 17 Presentations and documents

Cover: slide master layouts (title, section, content, two-column, image, quote, data, end), 16:9 safe areas, at least 24pt slide text, one idea per slide, accessible PowerPoint/Keynote/Google Slides (use layouts and placeholders so the reading order works, unique slide titles, alt text, no text in images, built-in accessibility checker), document templates (reports, proposals, letters, policy documents) with real styles (Heading 1–3, lists, table header rows), PDF export as **tagged PDF** with title, language and bookmarks, checked with an accessibility checker, file naming, and version control.

## 18 Signage, environment and merchandise

Cover: exterior and interior signs (logo size relative to viewing distance, contrast at least 70% light reflectance value (LRV) difference where building regulations apply, sans-serif, sentence case, mounting heights per local regulations, tactile and braille signs where required, e.g. ADA in the US and BS 8300 / Approved Document M in the UK; name only what applies and recommend checking), wayfinding pictograms (standard symbols, ISO 7001), office branding (reception wall, manifestations on glass doors for visibility), events (banners, roll-ups at 850×2000mm or 33×79in, lanyards, name badges at least 24pt names), vehicles, uniforms and embroidery (simplified logo, thread colours to match), merchandise (quality standards, sustainable sourcing, minimum logo size, single-colour printing), and packaging if relevant (regulatory text, legibility).

## 19 Co-branding and partnerships

Cover: relationship types (partnership of equals, sponsorship, endorsed sub-brand, supplier), lock-up rules (a divider line or "in partnership with", equal optical size for equal partners, our logo's clear space applied to the whole lock-up, order and alignment), using partner logos (their guidelines prevail for their mark), sub-brands and product brands (naming architecture), approvals and lead times, and accessibility (both logos still 3:1, alt text naming both organisations).
