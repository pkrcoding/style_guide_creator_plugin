# Social media guidance

## Accessible social posts: checklist

- [ ] Every image has alt text in the platform's alt field, describing the purpose (≤ ~125 characters for simple images; longer for infographics, plus a link to a full text version).
- [ ] Text in the image is also in the caption or alt text.
- [ ] On-image text has at least 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 for very large display text), is set in brand type, and sits inside the safe zone.
- [ ] Videos have accurate captions (reviewed, not raw auto-captions). Burned-in captions use at least 4.5:1 contrast, a solid or semi-opaque box, and sit above platform UI.
- [ ] Videos work with the sound off. Spoken content is captioned and key visuals are described where they carry meaning.
- [ ] No flashing (more than 3 per second). Warn before strobing content.
- [ ] Hashtags in CamelCase (#AccessibleDesign), at the end of the post.
- [ ] Emoji: at most 3, never in place of words, never repeated in strings, at the end of sentences.
- [ ] No Unicode "fonts" (𝐛𝐨𝐥𝐝, 𝘪𝘵𝘢𝘭𝘪𝘤, 𝔣𝔞𝔫𝔠𝔶). Screen readers cannot read them.
- [ ] Links are described ("Read the report: [link]"). Shortened links say where they go.
- [ ] Plain language. Acronyms spelled out.
- [ ] GIFs: short, no flashing, with alt text. Consider whether the GIF adds meaning.

## Hashtag guidance by platform (guidance only)

| Platform | Suggested count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 3–5 | In the caption, at the end |
| LinkedIn | 3–5 | At the end |
| X | 1–2 | Inline only if natural |
| TikTok | 3–5 | Mix brand and topic tags |
| Facebook | 0–2 | Low value |
| YouTube | 2–3 | In the description; the first three may show above the title |

## Caption structure

1. **Hook** (first ~125 characters; most platforms truncate after this).
2. **Value:** the point, in plain language.
3. **Call to action:** one, specific.
4. **Hashtags and mentions** at the end.

## Community management

| Situation | Response time (guidance) | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Question | Same business day | Answer or point to help; move personal details to a private channel |
| Complaint | Within 4 business hours | Acknowledge, apologise where appropriate, take it private, follow up publicly if resolved |
| Misinformation about us | Same day | Correct calmly with a source |
| Abuse, hate, harassment | Immediately | Hide or remove per moderation policy, report, never engage |
| Crisis | Per crisis protocol | Pause scheduled content, use the holding statement, follow the approval chain |

## Disclosure

Paid partnerships, gifted products and affiliate links must be clearly disclosed at the start of the post, using the platform's paid-partnership label where available and local advertising rules (for example the UK ASA/CAP code or US FTC Endorsement Guides). The guide should name the rules that apply to the organisation's markets, and recommend legal review.

## Account security

- Use 2FA on every account, with a business password manager and role-based access (no shared personal logins).
- Offboard access the day someone leaves. Review admins every quarter.
- Keep a register of all official accounts, owners and recovery details.
